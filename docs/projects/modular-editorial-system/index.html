Editorial Workflow:
From 2 Weeks to 20 Minutes

Contributions

    Context

    Olive Young Magazine was designed to drive user engagement and increase visits.

    Olive Young’s online mall used magazine content to deepen customer engagement and create new sales and marketing opportunities beyond product listings.

    Problem

    However, publishing a single article took almost two weeks.

    Making it hard to scale.

    Why It Mattered

    Why the long process became a problem

    Business Cost

    Missed sales and marketing opportunities

    • 01

      Slower response to trends

    • 02

      Less timely, relevant content

    • 03

      Lower engagement potential

    Operational Cost

    Scaling required more manpower

    • 01

      High dependency on supporting teams

    • 02

      Frequent ad-hoc requests for supporting teams

    • 03

      Repeated production costs

    Affected teams

    Who was struggling?

    Content Editors

    Primary
    • Could write, but not publish independently
    • Relied on designers and publishers for every article

    Designers & Publishers

    • Faced frequent ad-hoc interruptions
    • Handled repetitive production requests

    Core idea

    What if content editors could create, edit, and publish articles — all in one tool?

    A tool to make content creation faster, easier, and more flexible.

    Simplified process with consistent quality

    Intuitive UI/UX for non-technical users

    Immediacy
    in delivery

    Goal

    Provide an intuitive editorial tool that connects creative ideas with real-time publishing

    Strategy & Hypotheses

    Structuring for
    speed and consistency

    1. Build articles with reusable modules

    Reusable blocks would help editors assemble articles faster and publish more often without design or development support.

    2. Keep controls and assets consistent

    Shared editing controls and design assets would help maintain consistent quality across articles.

    3. Make editing easy to understand

    A clear, self-explanatory interface would reduce training time and day-to-day effort for non-technical editors.

    Success Metrics

    Defining impact

    1. Production Speed

    2. Publishing Volume

    3. Independence

    Reflection

    Make the case for usability
    in internal tools.

    Challenge

    The team faced pressure to finish within a month and prioritize speed over usability because this was an internal tool.

    Learning

    I learned to make the value of usability clear to stakeholders. Sharing our vision and discussing concerns helped build support for a tool that editors could use independently.
