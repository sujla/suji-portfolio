Pickup Order
UX Enhancement

E-commerce•Data-driven Design•Conversion Optimization

Project

Dec 2024 – Jan 2025

Role

UI/UX Design Lead

Team

  • 1 Product Manager
  • 2 Software Engineers

Platform

Mobile Web

After the store finder renewal, customers were checking stock but missing the next step. I made pickup actions easier to find and clarified which stores and products supported pickup.

Contributions

    Problem

    Customers wanted pickup.
    The interface sent them elsewhere.

    Funnel and event-stream data showed customers opening store details, then returning to the stock-checking page within one or two minutes. This suggested a mismatch between what they expected and what the store card did.

    01 · Easy to miss

    The small, pale pickup icon did not look like a clear next step.

    02 · Easy to misread

    The store card looked like a path to pickup, but opened store information.

    03 · Hard to decide

    Pickup eligibility was hidden, and missing buttons had no explanation.

    Goals

    Make the next step obvious.

    Help customers move from checking stock to ordering pickup without unnecessary detours.

    Show the action

    Make pickup recognizable at a glance.

    Show eligibility

    Surface available pickup options in the list.

    Explain limits

    Tell customers why pickup is unavailable.

    Final Solution · 01

    Give pickup a clear label.

    Replace the subtle bag icon with an explicit pickup button, so customers can recognize the action before tapping.

    Final Solution · 02

    Show where pickup is possible.

    Tailor store cards to stock and pickup eligibility. Add a pickup filter so customers can find eligible stores directly from the list.

    Final Solution · 03

    Explain why pickup is unavailable.

    For in-store-only products, replace the unexplained missing button with a clear purchase restriction message.

    Success Metrics

    Measure orders, not just clicks.

    Track whether a clearer pickup action translates into completed orders and business value.

    Result

    Small friction. Meaningful impact.

    Pickup conversion improved, while total pickup order volume and value both rose by more than 20%.

    Reflection

    Clearer actions can uncover demand.

    This project showed me how small points of confusion can stand between purchase intent and an order. Following unexpected behavior in the data helped reveal an opportunity that a broader redesign had missed.
